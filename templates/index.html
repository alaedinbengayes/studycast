How it works

  1. When you press the button, your five inputs are sent to the website's server.
  2. The server loads model.joblib, the scikit-learn pipeline trained in our Google Colab notebook. It fills any blank with the training median, one-hot encodes Access to resources, and applies a linear regression model.
  3. The model was trained on 80% of 6,606 student records and tested on the other 20%.

About

  • Dataset: Student Performance Factors – Kaggle
  • Tools: Python, pandas, scikit-learn, Google Colab, Flask, Vercel.
